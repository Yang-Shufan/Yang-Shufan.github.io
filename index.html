About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Public Policy at the School of Public Policy and Management, Tsinghua University. My research focuses on state–citizen interaction and government action in the age of digital and intelligent technologies.

I am particularly interested in how information communicated by governments shapes citizens’ perceptions and choices, and how public officials interpret, prioritize, and respond to citizens’ demands. I also examine how digital technologies reshape government behavior, organizational practices, and intergovernmental coordination.

Methodologically, I am especially interested in experimental methods, AI-assisted text coding and measurement, and the integration of artificial intelligence into experimental research.
